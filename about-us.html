About Us

Donahue Publishing was founded in 2020 by Joseph Donahue after struggling to publish his own manuscript. Frustrated by confusing processes and a lack of support, he set out to create a better path for authors. Built on transparency, respect, and care, the company offers expert guidance at every stage. Today, Donahue Publishing has helped over 1,200 writers across North America bring their books to life. What began as one author’s challenge is now a mission to empower every writer’s voice.

Our Team

Donahue Publishing is powered by a team of dedicated professionals committed to author success. From Author Concierges and Executive Publishers to Editorial and Creative Directors, each expert brings specialized skills to guide your book from manuscript to market. Our Digital Artists and SEO Experts ensure your book stands out visually and online. Together, we deliver a high-quality, personalized publishing experience at every step.

Our Approach

We believe publishing should be a collaborative, empowering experience. Our author-first approach offers expert support while respecting your creative vision. By understanding your goals, we tailor a journey that combines personal attention with professional quality. With experience in editing, design, marketing, and distribution, our team ensures your book meets the highest standards.

Our Mission

We empower independent authors with expert publishing services that honor their vision and deliver professional quality, distribution, and visibility. With integrity and transparency at our core, we turn manuscripts into books authors are proud to share.

OUR SERVICES

With our exceptional book writing services, we're here to support you at every stage of your book journey. At Donahue Publishing, we have the expertise to bring your book to life and captivate readers globally.

Ghostwriting

Let our ghostwriters turn your vision into a reality. Working behind the scenes, they’ll craft your story with precision, capturing your voice and ideas as if it were their own. Whether it’s fiction, memoir, or non-fiction, our talented team will create an authentic manuscript that feels like you wrote every word.

Book Editing

A great story needs a sharp editor. Our skilled editors will fine-tune your manuscript, honing the plot, strengthening characters, and smoothing the flow. With careful attention to detail, we’ll ensure your book is polished to perfection, ready to fascinate readers.

Cover Designing

First impressions matter, especially when it comes to books. Our expert designers will create a cover that not only stands out but also reflects the heart of your story. At a glance, your visuals will attract readers and make an unforgettable impression.

Book Coaching

Writing a book can be a long and challenging journey, but you don’t have to go it alone. Our experienced book coaches will be there every step of the way, offering guidance, motivation, and constructive feedback. Together, we’ll turn your writing goals into a finished product.

Book Marketing

In a crowded book market, standing out is key. Our strategic marketing services will create buzz around your book, reaching your target audience through personalized campaigns designed to boost visibility and engagement. Watch your readership grow with our expert marketing team. z

Illustration Design

Let your book’s visuals match its story. Our talented illustrators will bring your words to life with stunning artwork that enhances your narrative and appeals to readers of all ages. Make your book unforgettable with our unique illustration services.

Beta Reader Testing

Get real feedback before going to print. Our beta reader testing service taps into diverse groups of readers to provide honest insights and suggestions, helping you fine-tune your manuscript for maximum impact. Make your book the best it can be with reader-driven improvements.
