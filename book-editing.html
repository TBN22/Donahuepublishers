Why Our Editing Approach Makes Sense

The traditional editing industry has created unnecessary complexity and confusion. Many services require authors to:

  • Choose between multiple editing "levels" without fully understanding the differences
  • Pay separate fees for developmental, line, copy, and proofreading services
  • Navigate confusing per-word pricing that makes budgeting unpredictable
  • Worry about word count limits and extra charges for longer manuscripts
  • Make difficult decisions about which editing services they can "afford" to skip

    • At Donahue Publishing, we believe this approach fundamentally misunderstands what authors need. Your manuscript deserves all the necessary editing to make it exceptional—not just the editing types that fit your budget.

    Our Comprehensive Editing Package
    Strengthening the Foundation

    Our developmental editing focuses on the big-picture elements that shape your manuscript. We evaluate overall structure, pacing, and narrative flow to ensure a compelling journey for readers. For fiction, we refine character development and consistency, while for non-fiction, we enhance argument coherence and logical progression

    Refining Your Prose

    Line editing transforms your writing by elevating it at the paragraph and sentence level. We improve sentence structure, paragraph transitions, and overall flow, while enhancing clarity and readability. Redundancies and wordiness are eliminated, your voice and tone are strengthened, and language is adjusted for your intended audience. Often seen as a luxury add-on, we consider line editing a vital step that turns good writing into truly great prose.

    Perfecting the Details

    Our copy editors focus on the technical precision of your manuscript. This includes correcting grammar, punctuation, and spelling, ensuring consistency in hyphenation, capitalization, and terminology, and properly formatting numbers, dates, and citations. We also align your manuscript with the appropriate style guide, whether it's Chicago, APA, or another standard. While many services offer only this level of “basic” editing, we understand that technical accuracy is just one part of a professionally edited book.

    The Final Polishing

    As the last step before publication, our proofreading process ensures your manuscript is error-free and presentation-ready. We catch any lingering mistakes, check for consistency across all elements, and review formatting and layout. This stage also verifies that all previous editing changes have been applied correctly. Where many authors are forced to choose between copy editing or proofreading, we believe both are essential for delivering a final product that meets professional publishing standards.
