Our Complete Publishing Package
Custom Interior Formatting

The interior layout of your book affects readability and reader experience. Our formatting specialists create clean and professional layouts for both print and digital formats, ensuring properly formatted chapter headings, page numbers, and margins. We provide special treatment for images, quotes, and other distinctive elements, maintaining consistent typesetting that enhances readability. We follow industry best practices while adapting to the specific needs of your genre and content.

Eye-Catching Cover Design

Your book cover is your most important marketing tool. Our experienced cover designers create custom covers that reflect your book's theme and genre, using professional artwork and typography that stands out in online thumbnails. Each design is optimized for both digital displays and print editions, with cover variations available for different publishing platforms and marketing needs. We present multiple concepts and offer revision opportunities to ensure you're completely satisfied with the final result.

ISBN Registration and Copyright Filing

Protect your work and establish your book’s official identity with our ISBN registration and copyright filing services. We handle the registration of your unique ISBN for global book identification, assist with Library of Congress filing and cataloging information, and guide you through copyright registration. We also generate barcodes necessary for retail sales, ensuring that all technical details are covered for proper registration and protection of your work.

Print and Digital Preparation

Today's readers consume books across multiple formats, and we prepare your book for each one with precision. We set up your book for print-on-demand publication, Kindle and e-reader formats with proper navigation, and large print editions for accessibility. Audiobook production coordination is also available as an optional service. Every format is carefully optimized and tested to ensure quality, readability, and functionality across all major reading platforms.

Our Publishing Process

Editing, Design, and Formatting

Our editors polish your manuscript while preserving your voice, with multiple reviews to keep you in control. Our design team then creates a standout cover and professional interior layout, involving you at every key stage.

Post-Publication Support

After launch, we stay by your side with ongoing support, troubleshooting, and marketing guidance. Whether you need updates, advice, or next steps for growing your book’s impact, we’re here to help you navigate your post-publication journey.

Consultation and Manuscript Assessment

We start with a detailed call to discuss your manuscript, target audience, and publishing goals. We answer your questions, set clear timeline expectations, and recommend the best publishing approach for your book. Our editors then conduct a thorough manuscript review, providing actionable feedback to create a customized publishing plan.

Technical Preparation and Publication

We manage all technical essentials, including ISBN assignment, copyright registration, metadata optimization, and format conversions for each platform. When your book is ready, we coordinate its release across Amazon Kindle, Barnes & Noble, Apple Books, Google Books, and more for maximum reach.
