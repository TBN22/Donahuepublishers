Who We Are

At Donahue Publishing, we believe everyone has a story worth sharing. For years, we've been the trusted partner for aspiring and experienced authors alike, guiding them through every step of the publishing journey.

We're not a massive corporate publisher looking to churn out bestsellers; we're a dedicated team of publishing professionals who understand the personal nature of your work and treat it with the care and attention it deserves.

Founded by authors who experienced firsthand the challenges of getting published, we've built Donahue Publishing on a simple premise: making the publishing process accessible, transparent, and rewarding for independent writers. Our team brings decades of combined experience in writing, editing, design, distribution, and marketing—all under one roof to serve your unique vision.

WHAT WE DO
Ghostwriting
& Editing

Have a brilliant idea but struggling with putting it on paper? Our experienced ghostwriters and editors work closely with you to capture your voice and bring your vision to life. Whether you need help refining an existing manuscript or creating one from scratch, we ensure your story shines through every word. Our comprehensive editing services include developmental editing, line editing, copyediting, and proofreading—all available as part of our flat-fee packages

Custom Book
Cover Design

Beyond basic cover creation, our design team develops strategic, market-appropriate covers that both capture your vision and appeal to your target readers. We provide multiple design concepts, unlimited revisions, and formats optimized for all publishing platforms and marketing needs, ensuring your book makes the powerful first impression it deserves.

Children's Book
Illustration

For authors of children's literature, our specialized illustration team creates engaging, age-appropriate artwork that brings your story to life. From character development and storyboarding to final full-color illustrations, we provide comprehensive visual storytelling services designed specifically for young readers at every developmental stage.

Professional
Publishing

Your book deserves a professional touch. Our publishing services include expert formatting, captivating cover design, ISBN registration, copyright filing, and all the technical details that transform your manuscript into a polished, market-ready book that stands shoulder-to-shoulder with traditionally published works. We handle the complex formatting requirements for both print and digital formats, ensuring your book looks professional across all platforms.

Wide Distribution

Today's readers discover books across multiple platforms. We make your work available where readers shop—Amazon Kindle, Barnes & Noble, Apple Books, Google Books, Chapters, and more. With our print-on-demand capability, your book is also available in physical form without requiring expensive print runs or inventory management. Our distribution services include strategic metadata optimization to improve your book's discoverability in online searches.

Strategic Book Marketing

Writing your book is just the beginning. Our targeted marketing strategies help your book find its audience. From social media campaigns and author websites to book trailers and review solicitation, we create customized promotion plans that match your goals and budget. Our multi-channel marketing approach includes digital strategies, social media, traditional media placement, and in-person promotional opportunities.

Author Platform Development

Building your presence as an author is crucial for long-term success. We help develop professional author websites, email newsletter systems, social media strategies, and content marketing plans that establish your authority and build lasting relationships with readers.

Complete Launch Support

When your book is ready to meet the world, we're by your side. Our launch packages include coordinated release timing, promotional events, press releases, and the critical early steps that give your book momentum in the marketplace. We provide detailed launch timelines, marketing calendars, and strategic guidance to maximize your book's impact during this crucial period. Our relationship doesn't end when your book is published. We provide continued guidance on marketing, future editions, sequel planning, and additional publishing opportunities. Many of our authors return for multiple book projects, relying on our expertise throughout their writing careers.
